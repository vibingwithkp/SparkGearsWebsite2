Who we are

Student mentors, building access.

Spark Gears exists to make high-quality robotics education easier to start, easier to teach, and easier to share.

A nonprofit that hands out the playbook.

Spark Gears Initiative helps teams and community groups run free, hands-on robotics courses for youth ages 8 and up. We provide Arduino kits, lesson plans, slides, activities, and guides so local organizers can teach robotics without creating a full program from scratch.

Our work is grounded in practical learning. Students gain confidence through projects that teach programming, CAD, the engineering design process, and FTC-style competitive robotics, building, testing, troubleshooting, and explaining their work with student mentors alongside them.

What we believe

Three rules we teach by.

Learn by building

Students turn engineering ideas into working mechanisms, code, and presentations, and get confident by doing it.

Access first

Our courses are free and open to students of every background, so cost and experience are never the barrier.

Share what works

We publish our course materials free of charge so every lesson can be repeated, remixed, and improved.

Goals

What we're building toward.

Programs that communities can keep running on their own, not one-time demos.

  1. Accessible STEM

    Bring robotics, coding, CAD, and engineering design to students who might not otherwise have a structured technical program.

  2. Reusable curriculum

    Maintain Arduino kit guides, lesson plans, slides, activities, and event formats other groups can pick up and use.

  3. Local leadership

    Help robotics teams, STEM clubs, schools, and community organizations plan logistics, reach students, and teach in their own communities.

Mentor teams

Teams we support.

Beyond courses, we mentor younger and developing FTC teams on outreach, fundraising, and technical questions.

Dauntless Dragons

An FTC team from northern Virginia, engineering the future of robotics.

They are exactly the kind of student-led team we set out to support: learning design, programming, outreach, and competition readiness while growing as engineers and community leaders.

Autonomous Angels

Connected with us for outreach and fundraising mentorship.

Through mentorship calls we help them think through outreach planning, fundraising, program organization, and how to share robotics with a wider community.
