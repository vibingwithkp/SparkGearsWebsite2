May 2026 Hackathon

Code Crush Hackathon 2

Our second annual hackathon gave students 24 hours to build a game or project around a shared theme. Alongside the build, we ran bootcamps in marketing, AI/ML, Java, CAD, MVP pitching, and project presentation, so participants left stronger on both the technical and the communication side.

  • 24 hours
  • AI / ML
  • Java
  • CAD
  • Pitching
  • Marketing
Spring & Summer 2026 Course

Curie Autonomous Robotics Course

Students learned Arduino development, servo control, motor-based movement, sensors, and how to troubleshoot hardware and software. The course was built for students with no prior robotics, programming, or technical experience, and used project work to make abstract engineering ideas concrete.

  • Arduino
  • Servos & motors
  • Sensors
  • No experience needed
June 2025 Hackathon

Code Crush Hackathon

Our first annual hackathon gave students 24 hours to create a themed game or project. Before the build started, we ran introductory bootcamps in Python, Java, and AI so newer participants could learn the core tools and still present a finished project.

  • 24 hours
  • Python
  • Java
  • AI
August 2025 Camp

Curie Summer Camp

For one week, campers became their own mini FTC team. They learned about FIRST, built and programmed an FTC-style robot together, practiced working as a team, and finished by showing their robot to an audience.

  • 1 week
  • FTC-style robot
  • Teamwork
  • Final showcase
March 2025 Showcase

ACL Maker Showcase

At the Academies of Loudoun Maker Showcase, we introduced FIRST, competitive robotics, and our engineering process to educators, parents, and students. The booth had live robot demos, hardware and software walkthroughs, outreach displays, and plenty of conversations about how kids can get involved.

  • Live robot demos
  • FIRST
  • Families & educators
