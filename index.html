The gap

The guides exist. The help usually doesn't.

FIRST publishes great documentation, but a rookie team trying to run its first outreach event usually hits three walls:

  1. Supplies cost money

    Kits, parts, and printed materials come out of a budget most rookie teams don't have.

  2. Plans are generic

    Knowing outreach matters isn't the same as knowing exactly how to run a library station on a Saturday.

  3. No one to call

    When something breaks an hour before the event, there's no experienced team in your corner.

That's what we fill in, for free.
