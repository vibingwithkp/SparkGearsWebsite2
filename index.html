How hosting works

You bring the room. We bring the rest.

Host with us

  1. Find a space

    An FTC team, school, library, or STEM club provides a room, students, and a few hours a week.

  2. Get the kit

    We share Arduino kits, lesson plans, slides, activities, training, and volunteer verification.

  3. Students build

    Kids build, test, troubleshoot, and present real robots, with our student mentors backing you up.

Who we are

Student mentors, building access.

Spark Gears is a student-run nonprofit. We write the curriculum, assemble the kits, and mentor organizers, so local groups can run robotics programs without building one from scratch.
