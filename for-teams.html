Why outreach matters

It's good for your team, too.

  1. It counts

    Outreach is a core part of FIRST. It shows up in judged awards and in your engineering portfolio.

  2. It builds your network

    Running events in your community builds the local connections that lead to sponsors, recruits, and mentors.

  3. Rookies come first

    Rookie teams usually have the least time and money to do outreach. That's who we prioritize.

The roadmap

Five steps to event day.

You don't have to figure it out alone. A Spark Gears mentor walks through every step with your team.

Start step one

  1. Pick your event

    Library STEM station, school demo, intro robotics camp, or an Arduino workshop.

  2. Plan it with a mentor

    Have a Spark Gears mentor assigned to your team and have multiple meetings to plan, market, and set timelines for your event!

  3. Get the kit

    Supplies, slides, activity sheets, and a run-of-show for the day.

  4. Run it

    A mentor is on call during your event (in person when we can reach you in the DMV area).

  5. Log the impact

    We verify volunteer hours and help you capture photos and numbers for your portfolio.

Supply kits

What's in the kit.

Kits are limited each season. We prioritize rookie teams and Title I schools.

In the kit

  • Electronics, hardware, and parts for students at the event
  • Flyers, posters, and custom stickers for your team and event
  • Anything else you think should be in the kit

Who gets one first

  • Rookie FTC teams
  • Title I schools
  • Teams running their first outreach event
