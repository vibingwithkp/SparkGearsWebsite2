Host a program

A fair trade.

If you can provide a space, students, and time, we can cover almost everything else.

You bring

  • A space: classroom, library room, or team shop
  • Students who want to try robotics
  • Time to run sessions and handle local logistics
  • Someone to spread the word in your community

We bring

  • Free lesson plans, slides, activities, and guides
  • Arduino kits and kit guidance
  • Training so your instructors teach with confidence
  • Volunteer hour verification for your mentors

What you can run

Pick a format.

Built for FTC teams, schools, libraries, and STEM clubs.

FTC introductory camp

A weeklong camp where students form small teams, then build, program, and present an FTC-style robot. It's the format behind our Curie Summer Camp.

  • 1 week
  • Small teams
  • Final showcase

Arduino workshops

Short sessions covering lights, sensors, motion, debugging, and the basics of combining hardware and code. No experience needed.

  • Short sessions
  • Lights & sensors
  • Debugging

Materials & training

Already have a format in mind? We share course materials, kit guidance, and planning support so you can run it your way.

  • Lesson plans
  • Kit guidance
  • Planning support

Join the initiative

Anyone can join.

Robotics teams, STEM clubs, schools, and community groups across the United States are welcome. We provide full course materials free of charge. You plan the logistics, reach students, and teach in your own community.

Email Spark Gears Join the Discord

  1. Say hi

    Email us or drop into the Discord with where you are and who you want to teach.

  2. Plan it together

    We help you pick a format and get the materials and kits ready.

  3. Teach

    Run your program, and lean on our mentors whenever you need backup.
